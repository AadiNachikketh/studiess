## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for elite, high-stakes academic preparation. It translates the rigorous discipline of competitive exam preparation into a high-performance, developer-grade software interface. Rooted in the visual precision of modern SaaS benchmarks like Linear and Raycast, the experience merges hyper-efficient cognitive utility with an immersive, distraction-free atmosphere.

### Brand Character & Emotional Stance
- **Command & Control:** The interface feels like a mission telemetry deck rather than a casual study timer. It instills accountability, momentum, and operational clarity.
- **Deep Focus:** Uncompromising dark surfaces eliminate retinal fatigue over multi-hour problem-solving marathons.
- **Visual Velocity:** High contrast micro-accents provide immediate visual gratification for completed syllabus targets, active streaks, and mock performance deltas.

### Aesthetic Movement
The aesthetic synthesizes **Precision Minimal-Tech** with **Tonal Glassmorphism**:
- Deep cosmic slate canvas paired with structured tiered layers.
- Razor-sharp single-pixel border delineations with localized top-edge gradient luminescence.
- Dense information hierarchy elevated by clear visual breathing room and metric-first tabular typography.

## Colors

The palette uses a luminous deep-slate base complemented by electric spectral accents. Surfaces stack from deep background to elevated glass without washing out legibility.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas / Abyss:** `#080D1B` â€” Base canvas for the entire viewport.
- **Surface Level 1 (Panels / Base Containers):** `#111A2D` â€” Structural sidebars, content grouping modules.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Cards / Popovers):** `#16223B` â€” Foreground modules, hover states, metric monitors.
- **Border Default:** `#1E293B` â€” Structural hairline rules (1px).
- **Border Active / Highlight:** `#2B3954` â€” Focused containers, interactive hover rules.

### Functional & Domain Accents
- **Core Engine (Primary):** `#8B5CF6` (Electric Purple) â€” Brand identity, primary CTAs, active focus states.
- **Velocity (Secondary):** `#3B82F6` (Electric Azure) â€” Telemetry graphs, secondary links, active sessions.
- **Completion / Success:** `#22C55E` (Neon Emerald) â€” 100% syllabus masteries, accuracy spikes, solved markers.
- **Subject Specialization:**
  - **Physics (Electrodynamics / Waves):** `#06B6D4` (Cyan) to `#3B82F6` (Blue).
  - **Chemistry (Organic / Physical):** `#F59E0B` (Vibrant Amber) to `#F97316` (Neon Orange).
  - **Mathematics (Calculus / Algebra):** `#10B981` (Emerald) to `#14B8A6` (Teal).

## Typography

Typography prioritizes computational clarity and optical balance.

- **Display & Headings:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides geometric modernity with balanced counter-forms, avoiding sterile corporate rigidity while conveying disciplined authority.
- **Operational Data & Body:** `Inter` ensures flawless micro-legibility at 10pxâ€“14px sizes.
- **Tabular Figures & Metrics:** All numeric tallies, timers, percentile trackers, and rank monitors must activate OpenType tabular numbers (`tnum`) and slashed zero (`zero`) to prevent metric jitter during real-time updates.
- **Caps Hierarchy:** Micro labels and metadata chips make controlled use of uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.06em`) at sizes between 10px and 12px for immediate section scanning.

The system runs on a 4px/8px hybrid spacing grid optimized for high-density analytical dashboards.
